# Languages

The public site is available in **French** and **English**.

## How the language is chosen

- Every address starts with the language: `/fr/...` for French, `/en/...` for English. For example `/fr/galerie` and `/en/gallery` are the same page. You can share or bookmark an address in either language, and search engines index both.
- If you open the bare address of the site (no language), you are sent to the English or French home page according to your browser's language. French is used when your browser asks for neither.
- The site never stores your language choice: the address alone decides.

## Changing language

Use the **FR / EN** switch in the header. It takes you to the same page in the other language, and keeps any filters you chose (for example a category).

## Page not found

An address that does not exist shows a "Page not found" page in your language (in French when the language itself is unknown), with a link back to the home page.

## Texts not yet translated

When an artwork text has no English version yet, the French text is shown in its place. Screen readers pronounce it as French.

## Prices, sizes and dates

They follow the language of the page: `1 200 €` in French and `€1,200` in English; sizes read `H 62 × L 48 × P 20 cm` in French and `H 62 × W 48 × D 20 cm` in English.
