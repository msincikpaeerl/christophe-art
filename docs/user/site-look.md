# How the site looks

The site is designed like a contemporary gallery inside a workshop: light gallery walls for browsing the artworks, dark anthracite sections for the home page banner and the footer, and thin steel-coloured lines. The artworks are the only strong colours.

## What you will see on every page

- **Header**: the artist's name (a link to the home page), the main menu, and the **FR / EN** switch to change language. On a phone the menu goes on a second line.
- **Footer** (dark): the legal pages and the link to the artist's social profile.
- **Small details**: labels look like engraved metal plates (for example `H 62 × W 48 × D 20 cm`), a faint hexagon pattern (from the hex nuts of the sculptures) appears in the header, the footer and while content loads. It never appears behind an artwork.
- **Copper colour**: used only for links, buttons, prices and small details.

## Comfort and accessibility

- The site works on phones from 360 px wide, without sideways scrolling.
- Press the Tab key to move through links and buttons: the item you are on is always outlined in copper.
- If your device is set to reduce motion, the site shows no transitions or animations.
- Text colours meet the WCAG AA contrast level. Fonts are served by the site itself; no outside font service is contacted.

The full rules are in the [design system](../design.md).
